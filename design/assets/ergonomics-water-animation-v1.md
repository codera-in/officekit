## Prompt

Create a 16-frame LOOPING WATER BREAK animation sprite sheet, EXACTLY a 4 by 4 regular grid, square canvas, chronological row-major order. Use the reference character identity and LARGE bottle, but animate the pose rather than copying it. IMPORTANT ANATOMY: exactly TWO arms and TWO hands TOTAL on the character in EVERY frame. NO third arm. NO raised stretch arm. NO arch above its head. Top of its head is a smooth pear-shaped white blob only. The character has a small body, two feet, very large soft warm-white head, dark oval eyes, blush cheeks and ice-blue neckerchief, matte 3D clay aesthetic. The clear blue water bottle is LARGE, almost the height of the body plus head, has ONE single neck and ONE normal screw cap; no side neck or double opening. BOTH arms initially hold this bottle at belly height. Frames1-2 capped bottle upright. Frames3-5 ONE hand holds bottle while OTHER hand twists cap, then lifts the cap clear. Frame6 brings open neck to mouth, cap held in other hand. Frames7-10 drink by tilting bottle and head, cap still held. Frames11-12 lower bottle and smile happily. Frames13-15 replace and twist cap shut with the second hand. Frame16 same idle capped-bottle pose as frame1. Exactly two arms throughout, no extra detached limbs or ghost hands. Fixed camera, consistent head/body proportions, scale and foot baseline across all 16 cells. Full character AND bottle completely contained inside each cell with padding. Transparent background with REAL alpha, uniformly clean empty pixels outside subjects; no texture, no noise, no checkerboard, no shadows, no floor, no sparkles or visual sound effects. No text, no labels, no gridlines. No gutters. Designed to play as a 4-second looping tiny desktop companion animation.
